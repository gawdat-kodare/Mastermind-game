How to play

There are 7 colors, and 4 of them are correct. You must pay close attention to the correct colors, and the answer will appear in the right-hand section according to the following colors.

  • If the opposite color is red , this means that the color you selected does not exist.
  • If the opposite color is yellow , this means that the color you selected is present but in the wrong place.
  • If the opposite color is green , it means the color you selected is in the correct place.
